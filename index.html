ABOUT THE DESTINATION

A Wild Corner of North Sumatra

Bukit Lawang is a village beside Gunung Leuser National Park, known for its rainforest, rich biodiversity and welcoming local community.

Whether you are here for a jungle walk, wildlife watching or a peaceful riverside break, take time to travel thoughtfully and respect the natural environment.
